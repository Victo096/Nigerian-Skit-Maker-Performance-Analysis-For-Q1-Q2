# Nigerian-Skit-Maker-Performance-Analysis-For-Q1-Q2

The rapid growth of social media has transformed comedy and entertainment in Nigeria, giving skit makers a powerful platform to create content, build audiences, and generate substantial engagement. Facebook, in particular, provides measurable indicators such as views, likes, comments, and shares that can be used to understand how audiences interact with different creators and types of comedy content. Analyzing these indicators provides valuable insight into content performance, audience preferences, engagement patterns, and the relative reach of individual skit makers.

This analysis examines the **Facebook performance of selected Nigerian skit makers during the first and second quarters of the year, covering January to June**. The dataset was manually collated from Facebook and consists of **306 skits produced by seven Nigerian skit makers: Brain Jotter, Kiekie, Layi Wasabi, Mama Deola, Mr. Macaroni, Okola Baby, and Sabinus**. The six-month period provides a suitable basis for examining performance across creators, months, quarters, and content categories.

The study focuses on key performance metrics, including **views, likes, comments, shares, and the number of skits released**. In addition to evaluating individual creators, the skits were grouped into content categories to identify the themes that generated stronger audience engagement. Monthly and quarterly comparisons were also incorporated to reveal changes in content output and audience interaction over the period under review.

For the analytical process, **Microsoft Excel** was used for data preparation, cleaning, organization, and preliminary processing, while **Microsoft Power BI** was employed for data modelling, visualization, and the development of an interactive dashboard. These tools made it possible to transform the raw Facebook data into meaningful visual insights and provide a clearer comparison of performance across the selected creators.

Overall, the analysis aims to provide a **data-driven understanding of Nigerian skit performance on Facebook**, highlighting patterns in audience engagement, content consumption, creator performance, and skit categories between January and June. Rather than relying solely on popularity or perception, the study uses measurable social media indicators to demonstrate how audiences responded to the content produced by the seven selected skit makers during the period examined.


# Objectives of the Analysis

The primary objective of this analysis is to **evaluate and compare the performance of selected Nigerian skit makers on Facebook during the first and second quarters (January–June)** using key social-media engagement metrics. The analysis seeks to transform the data collected from 306 skits into meaningful insights that can support better understanding of content performance and audience engagement.

# The analysis aims to:

1. **Evaluate the overall performance of Nigerian skit makers** by examining key performance indicators such as total views, likes, comments, shares, and number of skits published.

2. **Compare the performance of individual skit makers** to determine how audience engagement is distributed among the selected creators.

3. **Analyze monthly engagement trends** from January to June  to identify periods of high and low audience interaction.

4. **Compare skit production across Q1 and Q2** to understand changes in the volume of content released during the two quarters.

5. **Examine the performance of different skit categories**, such as social, lifestyle, household, family, friendship, promotion, religion, relationship, culture, and university-related content.

6. **Identify the content categories generating the highest levels of engagement**, particularly in terms of likes and shares, to better understand audience content preferences.

7. **Assess audience interaction with individual creators** by comparing views, likes, comments, and shares and identifying differences in engagement patterns.

8. **Determine whether higher content output corresponds with higher audience engagement**, helping to distinguish between the quantity of skits produced and the level of engagement generated.

9. **Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard** that presents the findings in a clear and accessible format, allowing users to explore performance by creator, period, content category, and engagement metric.

10. **Generate data-driven insights that can support content strategy and decision-making** for skit makers, digital marketers, social-media managers, and other stakeholders interested in Nigeria's online entertainment industry.

# Key Insights from the Analysis of Nigeria Skit Maker Performance Analysis

Based on the Power BI dashboard covering **306 Nigerian skits published on Facebook between January to June**, the following key insights were identified:

1. **Strong overall audience reach and engagement:** The 306 skits generated approximately **378 million views, 42 million likes, 500,000 comments, and 222,000 shares**. This indicates substantial audience interaction with the selected skit makers during the six-month period.

2. **Kiekie recorded the highest number of views:** Kiekie generated approximately **114 million views**, representing about **30.06% of total views**. Sabinus followed closely with approximately **108 million views (28.49%)**, while Brain Jotter recorded about **95 million views (25.15%)**. Together, these three creators accounted for more than **83% of all views**, showing that viewership was highly concentrated among the leading creators.

3. **Kiekie dominated total likes:** Kiekie generated approximately **23 million likes**, accounting for about **55% of all likes** in the dataset. This is particularly significant because her share of likes was considerably higher than her share of total views, suggesting strong audience engagement with her content.

4. **Brain Jotter and Sabinus led in comments:** Brain Jotter recorded approximately **188,000 comments**, the highest among the analyzed skit makers, followed closely by Sabinus with approximately **177,000 comments**. This suggests that their content generated substantial audience discussion and interaction.

5. **Social and lifestyle content generated the strongest engagement:** The category analysis shows that **Social** content generated the highest number of likes, followed by **Lifestyle** content. Other categories such as household, family, friendship, promotion, religion, relationship, culture, and university-related content contributed considerably smaller volumes of likes and shares. This indicates that social and lifestyle themes were particularly prominent in audience engagement during the period analyzed.

6. **Likes declined substantially over the six-month period:** Monthly likes decreased from approximately **15 million in January** and **14 million in February** to **3.4 million in March**. Although there was a temporary recovery to approximately **5.4 million in April**, likes declined again to **1.9 million in May**, before increasing slightly to **2.2 million in June**. Therefore, audience likes were considerably stronger at the beginning of the year than toward the end of Q2.

7. **Q2 had more skit releases than Q1:** The quarter comparison indicates approximately **442 skits/content observations for Q2 compared with 306 for Q1** as displayed in the quarter visual. However, this appears inconsistent with the dashboard's KPI showing **306 total skits**, so the underlying quarter measure or aggregation should be checked before presenting this finding as a count of unique skits.

8. **High views did not always translate into the highest engagement across every metric:** The dashboard shows differences in creator rankings depending on the engagement measure being considered. For example, Kiekie led in likes and views, whereas Brain Jotter led in comments. This demonstrates the importance of assessing creator performance through multiple engagement indicators rather than relying solely on views.

## Overall Insight

The analysis shows that **audience engagement was concentrated among a relatively small number of creators and content categories**. Kiekie was particularly strong in views and likes, while Brain Jotter and Sabinus generated high levels of audience conversation through comments. Social and lifestyle content attracted the greatest volume of likes, while the monthly analysis reveals a notable decline in likes after the strong performance recorded in January and February. These findings suggest that **content theme, creator, and publication period all played important roles in the engagement patterns observed in the dataset**.


# Five Data-driven Recommendations for the Nigerian Skit Maker Performance

Based on the findings from the Nigerian Skit Maker Performance Analysis for January–June 2026, the following recommendations can help the creators improve engagement, strengthen their content strategies, and increase their earning potential.

1. Produce more high-performing content categories. Since Social and Lifestyle skits generated the highest volume of likes and engagement, creators should consider producing more content around relatable social situations, everyday Nigerian experiences, lifestyle, relationships, and current social trends. However, they should continue testing other categories rather than relying entirely on the two leading categories.
2. Focus on engagement, not just views. A large number of views does not automatically mean that a skit generates strong interaction. For example, the dashboard shows differences between the creators leading in views, likes, and comments. Creators should therefore track metrics such as engagement rate, comments per view, shares per view, and likes per view to understand which content actually connects most strongly with audiences.
3. Study the characteristics of top-performing creators. Kiekie's strong performance in views and likes, alongside Brain Jotter's and Sabinus's strong comment activity, provides opportunities for benchmarking. The analysis can be extended to examine factors such as content themes, video length, posting frequency, captions, collaborations, and posting time to identify practices associated with stronger engagement.
4. Address the decline in monthly engagement. Total likes dropped considerably after January and February. Creators should investigate whether this resulted from changes in posting frequency, content categories, audience interest, or individual creator activity. Identifying the cause can help them adjust their content calendars and avoid prolonged periods of declining engagement.
5. Use collaborations to expand audience reach. Skit makers can collaborate with other creators, musicians, actors, influencers, and brands. Collaborations can expose creators to new audiences while providing opportunities for jointly sponsored content.


# How the Skit Makers Can Earn More

The performance data can also be used to develop stronger monetization strategies. Rather than depending on a single income source, creators can combine several revenue channels.

Brand partnerships and sponsored skits can be particularly valuable. Creators with high views can demonstrate their reach to potential advertisers, while those with strong comments, likes, and shares can demonstrate audience engagement. They can prepare a professional media kit containing average views, engagement rates, audience information, content categories, and examples of successful campaigns when negotiating with brands.

Creators can also take advantage of Facebook's available creator monetization programs, subject to Meta's current eligibility requirements and policies. Rather than simply increasing the number of videos posted, they should focus on original, engaging content that attracts sustained viewing and interaction.

Product placement provides another revenue opportunity. Instead of producing advertisements that feel separate from their normal content, creators can naturally incorporate suitable products or services into their skits. For example, lifestyle-oriented skits could incorporate fashion, food, telecommunications, financial services, transportation, or consumer brands where appropriate.

Creators can further increase revenue through long-term brand ambassadorships. Strong and consistent performance data can help creators negotiate longer partnerships rather than relying exclusively on one-off sponsored posts. A creator who can demonstrate millions of views and consistent engagement may be able to present a stronger business case to potential partners.

Another opportunity is cross-platform distribution. Successful Facebook skits can be adapted and distributed through platforms such as Instagram, YouTube, and TikTok where appropriate. This can diversify audiences and reduce dependence on one platform for reach and revenue.

Finally, creators can develop their own products, services, events, or intellectual property. A strong online audience can potentially be converted into customers for merchandise, live comedy events, appearances, production services, memberships, or other creator-led businesses.
